Khepri's first release on the App Store.

New in this build:
- Your body on Today: the muscles you trained this week light up from your logged sets. Tap one to see when you last trained it and jump to its exercises.
- Muscles This Week in Training, with a front and back view.
- Workouts survive the app being closed, the rest timer notifies you when it ends, and you can log warm-ups and reps in reserve.
- Training weeks that fit your schedule: move sessions between days, rotate plans, or change one week only.
- Meal plans built around your macro targets, day by day.
- Weight and height can be updated from the Body sheet on Today.
