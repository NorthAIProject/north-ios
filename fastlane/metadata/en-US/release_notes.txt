Khepri's first release on the App Store.

New in this build:
- Build a meal plan with the coach: your goal, meals per day and the foods you won't eat, fitted to your macro target. Meals can be marked optional.
- Progress checks: photos and measurements on a schedule, first and latest side by side, and a body figure shaped by your latest check.
- Your week: a 7 or 30 day recap you can share or post to friends.
- Your coach can message you first, with answers right in the notification.
- Pick the weekdays you train; plans fit the time you have.
- A daily post on wellbeing and food, written by AI, under More.
- Khepri in Spanish and Portuguese, and a Light, Dark or System appearance setting.
