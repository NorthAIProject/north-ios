Khepri is a coach that knows you. It remembers what you told it last month, sees how you slept and trained, and helps you take the next small step toward the goals you set.

A COACH WITH MEMORY
Talk it through by text or voice. Khepri remembers what matters to you and what it learned from past conversations. You approve, pin or forget anything it keeps. Every week it writes you a short review of how things went.

TRAINING THAT FITS YOUR WEEK
Tell it your level, your equipment and your days, and get a plan built around them. Every exercise comes with an animated demonstration, cues and the muscles it works. Log sets, rest between them, and see your session on the Lock Screen while you train.

THE WHOLE DAY, NOT JUST THE WORKOUT
• Daily check-ins with a streak to keep
• Goals with milestones and progress
• Water, sleep, habits and reminders
• Meals, ingredients and your macro target
• A private journal
• Decisions you thought through, and how they turned out

CONNECTED
Workouts, steps, heart rate, sleep and weight come in from Apple Health, and rides and runs from Strava. Progress shows how each part of life is going, week by week.

OUTSIDE THE APP
Widgets for your next workout, your streak and today's water. Log a glass without opening the app. Say "Capture in Khepri" and log water, sleep or a check-in in one sentence.

YOUR DATA
Export everything whenever you like, or delete your account from Settings. Your health data is used to coach you and nothing else.
