Resubmission after the 5 October rejection (guideline 5.1.1(iv)): the microphone, Apple Health and notification explainers now have a single Continue button that always leads to the iOS permission prompt; none can be skipped or postponed, and none is labelled "Allow".

Sign in with the demo account above (email and password). It is seeded with a training plan, goals, check-ins and a coach conversation, so every tab has content.

Where to look:
- Today: the body figure shows the muscles trained in the last 7 days. Tap a highlighted muscle to see when it was last trained and open its exercises.
- Coach tab: ask anything, e.g. "How do I do a push-up?" to see the inline exercise card.
- Training tab: the plan, a day's exercises with animations, and "Start Workout" (Live Activity).
- Progress tab: charts from check-ins and, if connected, Apple Health.
- More: goals, check-ins, reports, memories, knowledge, nutrition, care, journal, decisions. Settings (More > Settings) holds Export My Data and, at the bottom, Delete Account.

HealthKit: used to read workouts, activity, heart rate, sleep and weight for coaching, and to save workouts finished in the app. Nothing is used for advertising.
Notifications: workout reminders are scheduled on the device; coaching nudges arrive by push.
Sign in with Apple and Google are offered alongside email.
