Sign in with the demo account above (email and password). It is seeded with a training plan, goals, check-ins and a coach conversation, so every tab has content.

Since the 5 October rejection (guideline 5.1.1(iv)), the microphone, Apple Health and notification explainers have a single Continue button that always leads to the iOS permission prompt; none can be skipped or postponed, and none is labelled "Allow".

Where to look:
- Today: the body figure shows the muscles trained in the last 7 days. Tap a highlighted muscle to see when it was last trained and open its exercises. "Your week" opens a 7 or 30 day recap that can be shared.
- Coach tab: ask anything, e.g. "How do I do a push-up?" to see the inline exercise card.
- Training tab: the plan, a day's exercises with animations, and "Start Workout" (Live Activity).
- Progress tab: charts from check-ins and, if connected, Apple Health.
- More > Nutrition > Plans > Build with AI: the coach builds a meal plan from a goal, meals per day and foods to leave out; it is shown for review before anything is saved.
- More > Progress check: photos and measurements. Photos are only sent for an estimate after the screen names the AI provider, and estimates are labelled as such.
- More > Daily Post: one short post a day, written by AI and marked "AI-written". Readers can only give kudos; there are no comments or user posts.
- More > Crews > "Review Crew": crew chat, the only user-generated content in the app (guideline 1.2). Crews are private and invite-only, up to 25 members; the demo account belongs to this crew with a second member and some messages.
  - Rules: the crew rules must be agreed ("I agree" is the only option) before the first message can be sent.
  - Filter: the server rejects messages that match a word list in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
  - Report: long-press any message > Report, with a reason and an optional note. The message is hidden for the reporter at once, and for everyone once 2 or 3 members report it, depending on crew size. Each report reaches the developer by push and is reviewed in a moderation queue, where content can be removed and the author suspended from posting, within 24 hours.
  - Block: long-press a message > Block, or from the member list. The author's messages disappear for you at once.
- More: goals, check-ins, reports, memories, knowledge, care, journal, decisions. Settings (More > Settings) holds Language, Appearance, Export My Data and, at the bottom, Delete Account.

HealthKit: used to read workouts, activity, heart rate, sleep and weight for coaching, and to save workouts finished in the app. Nothing is used for advertising.
Notifications: workout reminders are scheduled on the device; coaching nudges and coach messages arrive by push, and both can be turned off in Settings.
Sign in with Apple and Google are offered alongside email.
