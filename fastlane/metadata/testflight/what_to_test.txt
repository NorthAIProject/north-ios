Thanks for trying Khepri. New in this build, please try:
1. More > Nutrition > Plans > Build with AI: pick a goal, meals per day and foods to leave out, then review and save the plan.
2. More > Progress check: add a check with photos or measurements, then look at the body figure on Today.
3. Today > Your week: open the recap, switch to 30 days, and share it.
4. Training: generate a plan with the weekdays you choose.
5. More > Daily Post: read today's post, give kudos, tap Ask the Coach About This.
6. More > Settings > App: switch the language and the appearance.

Also worth a look: coach messages on Today, and answering one from the notification.

Anything confusing, broken or missing: use TestFlight's feedback (screenshot and send).
