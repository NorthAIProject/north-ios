Thanks for trying Khepri. New in this build, please try:
1. More > Crews: open a crew. It opens on Chat; agree to the crew rules, then send a message.
2. Long-press a message: react, reply, edit, pin, report, or block its author.
3. Leave the app and have a crew mate post: you get a notification, and Reply sends straight from it.
4. Check the unread badges on More, the Crews row and each crew.
5. A crew link (/app/crews/...) opens that crew's chat.

Anything confusing, broken or missing: use TestFlight's feedback (screenshot and send).
